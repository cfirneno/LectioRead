Replit shows you what records to add, but it can't add them for you unless the domain was purchased through Replit and using Replit nameservers.

Two scenarios:

You bought lectioread.com through Replit → DNS is managed for you. In that case, the records shown are already live; just wait a few minutes for Clerk to verify them. The status next to each record should turn green/"Verified" within minutes to an hour.

You bought it elsewhere (Namecheap, GoDaddy, Cloudflare, etc.) → Replit only shows you the records; you must log into that provider and add each CNAME there manually. Once added, return to the Replit Domains panel and the status will flip to Verified once propagation completes (typically minutes, up to 48 hours).

Check the status indicator next to each CNAME in the Domains panel. If they say "Pending" or "Unverified," DNS hasn't propagated yet — wait, or add them at your registrar. If they say "Verified," try the signup again; email codes should now deliver.
