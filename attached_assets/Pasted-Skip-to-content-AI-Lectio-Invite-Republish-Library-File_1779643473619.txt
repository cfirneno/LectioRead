"IMMEDIATE VERIFICATION required for lectioread.com"
"Verify your email address for lectioread.com"
"ICANN verification required"
Where to look:

Search your inbox for lectioread or verification.
Check Spam / Promotions / Junk — these registrar mails very often land there.
